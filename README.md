## 📖 Overview

**BRACU Iron Dome** puts you in the shoes of a missile-defense operator tasked with protecting **BRAC University** from incoming airstrikes. As enemy missiles rain down from the skies, you must react fast, track trajectories, launch interceptors, and keep the campus safe through increasingly chaotic waves of attack.

### 🎯 Key Objectives & Gameplay Features
* 🛰️ **Track & Target:** Swiftly detect incoming enemy strikes across the sky.
* 🚀 **Launch Interceptors:** Fire defensive missiles to destroy hostiles in mid-air.
* 💥 **Chain Combos:** Trigger tactical explosive chain reactions for bonus points.
* 🎯 **Ammunition Management:** Balance speed and precision—don't run out of ammo during critical barrages.
* 🏆 **High Score Chasing:** Survive relentless waves to achieve the highest possible score.

## 🎮 Levels & Difficulty Progression

The game features dynamic difficulty escalation across 3 distinct stages:

| Stage | Mission | Difficulty | Key Features |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 🟢 **Level 1** | **WARM-UP** | `Easy` | Basic controls, fewer missiles, target practice. |
| 🟠 **Level 2** | **BARRAGE** | `Medium` | Faster pace, frequent waves, chain-combo rewards. |
| 🔴 **Level 3** | **FINAL STRIKE** | `Hard` | Heavy strikes, special/mega missile alerts, intense pressure. |

<br/>

### 🟢 Level 1 — Warm-Up
> Get familiar with radar tracking and missile interception mechanics.
* Low missile count and lower descent speed
* Ample time to calibrate aiming and timing
* Focuses on understanding core game physics

### 🟠 Level 2 — Barrage
> The attack escalates—test your reflexes under pressure.
* Increased missile frequency and density
* Demands faster targeting and defensive reactions
* High incentive for building chain-combos to clear waves

### 🔴 Level 3 — Final Strike
> All-out aerial war over the campus. High alert!
* Massive missile density and accelerated fall speeds
* Sudden **Special / Mega Missile** warning events
* Strict defense management—requires strategic interceptor timing and ammo preservation
